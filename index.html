About Huroof (حروف) Arabic Series

Designed by experienced educator **Saaliha Al-Shaikh**, the Huroof Arabic Learning Series (Sanaabil / Sunbula 1 & 2) bridges traditional phonics with modern interactive video guidance. Every lesson incorporates clear Arabic calligraphic typography, vowel color coding, handwriting guidelines, and direct QR codes linking to step-by-step video lessons.
